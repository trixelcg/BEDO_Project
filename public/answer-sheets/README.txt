BEDO answer sheets — Measurement of Jet Forces (VL-FM009)

One worksheet per experiment, opened by the final step of the guided lesson.

Provenance: copied unmodified from BEDO's Phase 2 delivery,
"Measurement of Jet Forces/Phase 2/Exp.{1..4} (Answer sheet).pdf".
Mapping is by experiment id, not by file order:

  flat.pdf     <- Exp.1 (Answer sheet).pdf   Flat surface deflector
  semi.pdf     <- Exp.2 (Answer sheet).pdf   Semi-circular deflector
  conical.pdf  <- Exp.3 (Answer sheet).pdf   Conical surface deflector
  oblique.pdf  <- Exp.4 (Answer sheet).pdf   Oblique surface deflector

Despite the name these are not answer keys: each is a blank worksheet in which
the student computes Q, Vo, V^2, F_th and F_ac by hand and plots F against Q.
See docs/32 section 3 and docs/35.

They are fetched on demand when the learner opens the sheet — never at boot.
